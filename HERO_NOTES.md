# Hero Reference Analysis & Observations (`reference-shots/ref-1440-y0.png`)

1. **Background & Base Theme**: Absolute black (`#000000`) canvas with pure white text (`#FFFFFF`) and vibrant red accents (`#FF223A`).
2. **Top Header**: Left-aligned brand monogram/logo, right-aligned clean text navigation links (`Works`, `Services`, `About`, `Contact`).
3. **Huge Split Headline**: Stacked multi-line headline ("We spark web and / mobile experience") with italic serif emphasis on action words. For our site: "Sumit Anand" and "Eyes on every hub." with italic serif on "Anand" and "every hub.".
4. **Side-by-Side Panels (Design & Develop)**: Two large rectangular side-by-side cards directly below the headline in the hero section. Each has an index (`01`, `02`), uppercase category tag (`OPERATIONS`, `SYSTEMS`), title, subtitle, and hover transition state.
5. **Responsive Proportions**: Designed for 1440px desktop, 768px tablet, and 390px mobile viewports with fluid clamp typography and single-column stacking on mobile.
